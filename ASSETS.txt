Scientific media
----------------
The films and posters are newly generated portfolio demonstrations.
The optimisation film uses the public capstone query history; fitted surfaces
are labelled as models rather than true objective functions.
The flow film is an independent numerical demonstration, not an unpublished
research result. Reproduction packages document numerical methods and limits.
No thesis figures, thesis footage or private research datasets are distributed.
